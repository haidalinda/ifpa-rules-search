# IFPA Rules Search

Not an official IFPA or PAPA product. Everything runs on your device with no AI.

**Glossary.** Terms marked as quoted from the rules are word for word. The other meanings are written for this site and checked against the glossaries of the [Internet Pinball Machine Database](https://www.ipdb.org/glossary.php) and Wikipedia.
